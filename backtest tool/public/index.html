Your local research workflow

  1. Explore the clearly labeled synthetic demo.
  2. Import SPY one-second OHLCV CSV files. For Databento, select decimal prices and ISO timestamps with a timezone. Include the symbol field. DBN users can use the included conversion helper.
  3. Select dates, adjust strategy settings, and run a backtest.
  4. Click a trade to jump to its chart. Switch 1s / 1m / 5m, inspect levels, and review the report.
  5. Save presets and compare saved runs. Export CSV trades and complete JSON reports.

Five-minute zones become visible when their source candles close. A Zone candle may stop up to $0.05 short of the near zone edge; Zone and Reversal wicks may extend at most $0.05 beyond the far edge. Reversal must be the opposite color to Zone. Confirmation must close above the Zone demand area for calls or below the Zone supply area for puts. Entry triggers use the confirmation close plus $0.02 for calls or minus $0.02 for puts, during seconds 0–9 of the next minute. Entries and exits use one-second bars. Spread is the full assumed bid–ask width. Slippage is additional dollars per share per market execution. Target exits model limit fills without favorable price improvement.
