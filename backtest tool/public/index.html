Your local research workflow

  1. Explore the clearly labeled synthetic demo.
  2. Import SPY one-second OHLCV CSV files. For Databento, select decimal prices and ISO timestamps with a timezone. Include the symbol field. DBN users can use the included conversion helper.
  3. Select dates, adjust strategy settings, and run a backtest.
  4. Click a trade to jump to its chart. Switch 1s / 1m / 5m, inspect levels, and review the report.
  5. Save presets and compare saved runs. Export CSV trades and complete JSON reports.

Five-minute zones become visible when their source candles close. Entry decisions use completed one-minute candles; exits use one-second bars. Spread is the full assumed bid–ask width. Slippage is additional dollars per share per market execution. Target exits model limit fills without favorable price improvement.

Historical validation against the earlier Thinkorswim trade report remains necessary. The demo is synthetic and says nothing about strategy profitability. This application does not place orders.
