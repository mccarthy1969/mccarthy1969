Settings to vary and allowed values

Check a setting to vary it. Values are JSON arrays; null means no maximum. Trading-start choices here use Eastern exchange time.

Exit rules: use paths such as "exitStrategy.exits.0.cents": [10,20,30] or "exitStrategy.exits.1.moveStop.amount": [0.1,0.2,0.3]. Or supply complete valid plans under "exitStrategy": [...]. Exit percentages must still total 100%. Invalid combinations are skipped.

Sampled: broad search followed by variations around training leaders. Exhaustive: every valid unique combination plus baseline; up to 20,000 selected combinations. Budget and seed do not limit exhaustive coverage. Baseline always included. This is a bounded search, not a guarantee of a global optimum. A finalist must meet the annual minimum in all years to qualify; later results never change its training rank. Searches checkpoint after each session and are separate from the ten saved backtests.

Your local research workflow

  1. Explore the clearly labeled synthetic demo.
  2. Import SPY one-second OHLCV CSV files. For Databento, select decimal prices and ISO timestamps with a timezone. Include the symbol field. DBN users can use the included conversion helper.
  3. Select dates, adjust strategy settings, and run a backtest.
  4. Click a trade to jump to its chart. Switch 1s / 1m / 5m, inspect levels, and review the report.
  5. Save presets and compare saved runs. Export CSV trades and complete JSON reports.

Five-minute zones become visible when their source candles close. A Zone candle may stop up to $0.05 short of the near zone edge; Zone and Reversal wicks may extend at most $0.05 beyond the far edge. Reversal must be the opposite color to Zone. Confirmation must close green and above the Zone demand area for calls, or red and below the Zone supply area for puts. Doji candles cannot confirm. Entry triggers use the confirmation close plus $0.02 for calls or minus $0.02 for puts, during seconds 0–9 of the next minute. Entries and exits use one-second bars. Spread is the full assumed bid–ask width. Slippage is additional dollars per share per market execution. Target exits model limit fills without favorable price improvement.
