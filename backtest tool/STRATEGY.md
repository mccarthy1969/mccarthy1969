# Strategy mapping and simulation contract

## Baseline

The preserved source is `reference/ZRCE_STAGE_A_STRATEGY_v1.4_SIM_ONLY.txt`, recovered from the previous “Continue ZRCE Stage A strategy” chat's local deliverables. The supplied source's internal zones are ported here; its comment mentions a separate Major Zones V4.9 study, which was not available in that deliverable. This app displays every qualifying internal five-minute demand/supply zone using the same formation rules.

## Signals

1. Reset state and levels daily. Use SPY one-minute candles within the configured session.
2. At 5m boundaries starting ten minutes after open, inspect the two preceding *completed* five-minute candles. A red then green pair with the first close within meeting tolerance (default $0.03, inclusive, in either direction) of the second open proposes demand at their minimum. A green then red pair proposes supply at their maximum. Every qualifying candidate is retained and drawn through the session end. Demand keeps the lowest daily candidate and supply the highest as major-level references; only the currently active highest and lowest non-overlapping zones on each side are eligible for retests. Same-side overlapping or touching candidates are skipped. Interior candidates are inactive. Replaced zones retain their historical chart segments but cannot start or continue Z/R/C/E setups. A zone that retains either extreme role stays active. Zone width defaults to $0.05.
3. First retest only: each active zone has one opportunity, beginning on its first completed 1m candle whose range intersects the zone plus the existing $0.05 touch tolerance on either edge. This consumes the opportunity even if the candle fails the Z color/approach/penetration rules, the strategy is in cooldown, or another setup/position prevents entry. That first touch alone may start Z, followed immediately by R/C/E. A failed confirmation, entry-window expiry, data gap, entry/trend/session rejection, or successful trade never permits another attempt from the same zone. Formation candles are excluded because the zone becomes available only after they close. Spent zones remain drawn as reference levels, but cannot produce later Z markers or entries. Daily resets create a fresh zone set.

   Z long: a red minute opens above demand top, with its full-candle low between demand bottom - $0.05 and demand top + $0.05 inclusive. Z short: a green minute opens below supply bottom, with its full-candle high between supply bottom - $0.05 and supply top + $0.05 inclusive. The five-cent approach tolerance and five-cent maximum penetration include wicks and are independent of zone width.
4. R must be the immediately following opposite-color minute: green for long, red for short; a doji is not a reversal. Its low must be at or above the original Z demand bottom - $0.05 for long, or its high at or below the original Z supply top + $0.05 for short. It need not retouch the zone. C is the next minute and must close strictly above the original Z demand top for calls, or strictly below the original Z supply bottom for puts. C must close green (close > open) for CALLS or red (close < open) for PUTS; dojis cannot confirm. C need not exceed R's close. Failed R/C sequences use the inherited cooldown counter (10 + cooldownMinutes, decrement through state 10 before returning ready). E is the immediately following minute. Record C close and arm a trigger $0.02 above it for calls/longs, or $0.02 below it for puts/shorts. Enter on the first touch during E seconds 0–9 (E open <= timestamp < E open + 10 seconds); otherwise the setup expires. E may open on either side of C close. This replaces the original optional entry-direction filter; legacy directionGate preset values are ignored.
5. Stop is the cent-rounded extreme of the prior three minutes (configurable). Each zone independently tracks favorable completed 5m runs after formation or retest. CALLS require an active demand retest and PUTS require an active supply retest, followed by the same R/C confirmation rules. Active selection is evaluated before entry at each minute open; replacement cancels a pending setup immediately. Previously recorded valid markers remain historical, and open positions retain their existing exits. The selected zone supplies its own completed-run target. Completion stores the last favorable run close. A newly formed zone begins with no completed target. Target phases update at completed five-minute boundaries only, beginning at +10 minutes, as in the source. A target must be on the profitable side with reward/risk at least the configured minimum (default 1). Optional fixed-R targets are a research variant, not the baseline.
6. Stops and targets are cent-rounded. The trigger preserves C close precision plus/minus exactly $0.02 (normalized to eight decimals). Initial R/R and fixed-R targets use that trigger. A second R/R gate uses the actual cost-adjusted fill; a failed check consumes the setup. Only one position is held; opposite signals cannot reverse it. No new entries occur in the first 30 minutes or the final 60 minutes of the configured session. Entry timestamps must be at or after session open + 30 minutes and strictly before session close - 60 minutes. Date-specific early-close overrides set that cutoff; missing opening data does not shift it. Restricted setups are consumed rather than delayed. Zone and trend calculations continue during excluded entry periods, and existing trades keep their normal stop/target/session-close exits. An exit during a minute cannot cause another entry inside that minute.

## Fills and intentional differences from ThinkScript

- Signals use completed 1m candles; positions are evaluated using 1s candles, including the entry second. The engine never feeds future minute highs/lows to the opening decision.
- The engine checks one-second highs for long triggers and lows for short triggers, in chronological order. A crossing inside a second fills at the trigger reference; if that second opens beyond the trigger, the reference is the opening price. Entry time is the actual trigger-second timestamp, not the minute open. Spread/slippage are then applied as below.
- Records must be continuous from the E-minute opening second through the trigger. Missing seconds before entry reject the setup rather than inventing a missed fill. The 10-second deadline is elapsed time, never a count of available rows. Missing prior minutes also reject incomplete signal windows.
- Assumed full spread defaults to $0.02. Synthetic ask = reference trade price + half-spread; bid = reference − half-spread. Entry adds adverse per-side slippage to the buy/short execution. Trade prices are only an approximation to midpoint, not recovered historical quotes.
- Stop and target are executable exit-price thresholds. For a long, evaluate the synthetic bid OHLC; for a short, the ask. Consequently a spread-adjusted high may fail to reach a target that the trade-price high touched. Stops and session exits also incur additional adverse slippage; target limits do not.
- Resolve opening gaps before the rest of the second: gap through stop fills at executable open plus adverse slippage; favorable target gap fills at the target (no improvement). If no opening gap resolves the order and both levels touch in a second, close the simulated position and retain an **AMBIGUOUS** ledger record with no included P/L. This replaces ThinkScript v1.4's stop-first ambiguity rule at minute resolution.
- If entry triggers inside a second and that second also touches the stop, the ordering may be unknown even without a target touch. Retain an excluded AMBIGUOUS record. Do not apply that second's pre-entry opening price as an exit gap. Entries already triggered at a second's open use the normal exit rules.
- End-of-session liquidation uses the final observed close within the configured final minute. A missing entire final minute produces a DATA_END exclusion if a position remains. A position already exited normally earlier in a truncated day remains included, with the day flagged.
- Identical ambiguity exclusion rules apply to all parameters, with exclusions counted. Removing ambiguous observations can bias performance; their frequency is visible.
- Fees are per share per side; the report's “gross” is after modeled spread/slippage but before explicit fees. Net subtracts both side fees. Fixed shares apply equally to long and short. Capital is for reference return percentage, not portfolio leverage enforcement. No compounding, cash constraint, short borrow fees, dividends, interest, order queue, partial fills or option premium simulation. Stop/target touch is assumed sufficient for the modeled fill.
- No old ThinkScript packed-price <$1,000 ceiling: normal numeric prices replace packed registers.
- Spread scenarios can be compared by rerunning the same dates with saved $0.01/$0.02/$0.05 presets. The app does not label any of those as measured historical spreads.

Profit factor is null/displayed “—” when there are no losing dollars (rather than inventing a finite figure). Drawdown is closed-trade dollar drawdown, not mark-to-market drawdown. ## Chart VWAP and intraday trend study

This shared study supplies both chart labels and the strategy entry filter. VWAP resets at the configured session open and uses cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 times volume from the available one-second OHLCV bars, divided by cumulative volume. It is an OHLCV approximation, not tick-exact VWAP. The gold line samples that same calculation at each displayed candle's last observed second, so changing chart timeframe never changes the underlying study.

Trend now uses only session VWAP slope: (latest completed five-minute VWAP minus the VWAP five completed samples earlier) / 25 minutes. Six consecutive five-minute candles provide the 30-minute data window. Candle color, close location relative to VWAP, and high-low ranges do not influence the label. Slope above the positive threshold is Uptrend; below its negative is Downtrend; within the inclusive symmetric band is Sideways. The chart slider sets trendSlopeThreshold from $0.000 to $0.050 per minute in $0.001 steps, default $0.005/min (half a cent per minute). Higher thresholds classify more slopes as sideways; zero classifies only level VWAP as sideways. The setting is saved with presets and runs and applied to the entry filter.

Two consecutive five-minute evaluations must agree before the displayed classification changes. Hold the prior confirmed label during a one-update candidate change, and show the pending candidate in the hover explanation. The first evaluation occurs at +30 minutes and the first confirmation can occur at +35 minutes (10:05 for a 09:30 open). Missing five-minute bars or zero-volume windows immediately return Unclear and reset pending confirmations. Decisions use only data available at their timestamps; historical labels are never moved backward to the beginning of a trend.

The colored strip begins each classification at its availability time, never at the start of the candle used to compute it. Hover over the chart or strip to read the classification, reason, and slope at that time. Without hover, the readout describes the end of the visible candles. New reports archive their exact trend timeline and trend source hash alongside the signal annotations. Their chart uses the archived timeline. Older reports without archived trends display the currently calculated trend overlay without altering their original trades.

Adjusting the slider previews the current slope method immediately, preserving chart zoom and saved trades. A saved-run preview is explicitly labeled; run a new backtest to recalculate entries. Reopening a saved run restores its archived timeline and configuration.


## Named exit strategies (engine 0.11.0)

The top of the research sidebar selects Original single target or a named custom strategy. Add/remove exits and save the library in this browser. Preset JSON and saved runs also include the selected strategy definition. Every exit has a positive percentage of the ORIGINAL position, and all percentages must total 100%. Whole shares are allocated by largest fractional remainder, with ties following configured exit order; every exit must receive at least one share or the setup is rejected. Existing SPY-share execution and P&L apply; these settings do not add an option-premium pricing model.

Target types: a positive number of cents from the actual cost-adjusted entry, or the meeting price / outer wick of an opposing completed candle pair on 1m or 5m. Zone-pair color and meeting tolerance follow existing formation rules, with session-aligned candles. A CALL uses supply; a PUT uses demand. Each candle target selects the nearest profitable matching level confirmed at or before the entry signal. Max is the high of both source candles for CALLS or low of both for PUTS. Missing levels reject the setup. Targets remain fixed after entry. The source catalog includes confirmed opposing pairs regardless of the entry-zone extreme filter. No future candles can resolve an earlier target.

The minimum R/R gate uses the quantity-weighted planned reward, first at the signal and again after actual fill and whole-share allocation. Custom strategies replace the original targetMode/fixedRR controls. Targets execute from nearest to farthest, regardless of editor row order. A favorable opening gap can fill multiple targets at their limit prices. The original stop closes only remaining shares. Stops, session exits and incomplete-data handling retain existing semantics. Unknown stop/target order within a second excludes the whole trade; actual earlier partial fills remain recorded for inspection. Entry fees plus exit fees cover the original quantity once, with no duplicate fee for each TP. Summary metrics count one position as one trade, with P&L aggregated across its partial exits. Equity is posted when that position finishes.

Reports retain exitPlan and individual exits (time, price, quantity, realized percentage, reason), plus an exit-module fingerprint. The ledger's exit price is the share-weighted average; SCALE_OUT means multiple fills. Selecting a trade shows all fills, and the chart draws each TP and exit. CSV includes the individual fills as a quoted JSON column. Legacy saved reports keep their original data.

## 1m body-turnaround targets (engine 0.12.0)

For 1mMeet exits only, any consecutive completed green/red pair defines a CALL-side body turnaround, and red/green defines a PUT-side body turnaround. No close/open matching tolerance applies. Compare the first candle close and the second candle open to the actual entry and choose the body price with the smaller absolute distance (ties choose the first close). Wicks do not affect this target. A pair whose chosen body price is not profitable is ineligible; do not substitute its farther body price. Select the nearest eligible pre-entry turnaround. Both candles must have closed before the entry signal. Existing 1mMax and 5m target rules and entry-zone formation tolerance remain as previously defined.

## Entry-zone excursion for 1m meet (engine 0.13.0)

1mMeet now scopes its turnaround search to the preceding move away from the SAME entry zone. Find the last tolerance-adjusted touch before the current Z candle, searching back through the zone’s ten-minute source formation window. Include that departure candle; consider only completed opposing 1m pairs from that point through the start of the current Z. Select the highest body turnaround for CALLS or lowest for PUTS, then choose that pair’s first close or second open closest to entry. Do not select unrelated session turnarounds or turns during the current Z/R/C. Absence of an eligible previous excursion rejects the setup. Other exit types retain their existing behavior.

## Meet-target early-exit margin (engine 0.14.0)

Both 1mMeet and 5mMeet exits use a fixed $0.05 margin toward entry: executable target = rounded candle body level minus direction * $0.05. CALL targets move down five cents and PUT targets move up five cents. Fixed-cent and Max targets are unchanged. Each exit plan retains rawTarget and margin alongside the actual executable target. Risk/reward checks, displayed TP lines, fills, and P&L use the adjusted target. Reject a setup if the adjusted target is at or beyond entry in the losing direction. Existing spread-aware executable-price and intrasecond ambiguity rules still apply.

## Stop actions after partial exits (engine 0.15.0)

Each custom exit may specify moveStop: omitted/keep, breakEven, or offset with a positive dollar amount. Break even means the actual original entry fill, before fees. Offset is a one-time placement behind the current SPY trade price at that exit: CALL stop = SPY - amount, PUT stop = SPY + amount, cent rounded. For intrabar limit touches, SPY is the target executable price converted back by half-spread; for opening-gap fills, use that second’s actual SPY open. Do not use the later candle close or high/low to set the stop. Stops only tighten; a requested move that increases risk is ignored. This is not continuous trailing.

Only remaining shares are affected; the final exit cannot move a closed position’s stop. Changes take effect starting with the next one-second sample, avoiding unknown same-second ordering around a TP fill. Multiple moves from the same sample combine into the tightest stop. Existing spread/slippage, ambiguity and gap-stop execution rules apply to the adjusted stop. Reports retain initialStop and stopHistory, and partial-fill details include stopAfter when a change actually became effective. The chart draws the stop’s historical segments; CSV includes original stop and change history. Existing strategies without moveStop retain their behavior.

## Trailing stops after exits (engine 0.16.0)

A per-exit moveStop type trailing accepts a positive dollar amount, defaults to $0.20 in the editor, and activates only when that TP fills with shares remaining. It tracks the highest observed SPY price after activation for CALLS and the lowest for PUTS, placing the stop that dollar amount behind the favorable extreme. The fill initializes the anchor; each completed one-second sample then updates it with that second’s favorable high/low. Tightening takes effect on the next sample, retaining the existing no-same-second-lookahead stop policy. Stops never loosen. The original offset action remains a one-time move. A later keep action retains an active trail; a later breakEven or offset action ends trailing and applies its one-time rule without loosening the current stop. A later trailing action replaces its distance and reanchors at that exit, without loosening the current stop. No trail is activated after the final shares exit. StopHistory, charts, saved exit strategies, presets, reports and CSV retain these actions.

## Maximum zone-to-entry distance (engine 0.17.0)

maxEntryDistance defaults to null (no maximum). The Strategy rules field accepts a non-negative dollar amount; blank disables the limit. For the actual cost-adjusted entry fill, measure the absolute distance to the nearest edge of the original retested zone (zero inside the zone). A $0.30 limit admits an entry exactly $0.30 away and rejects one beyond it, allowing only numerical precision tolerance. This applies to CALLS/PUTS, original single-target and custom exit strategies. Check the real trigger/gap fill including spread and slippage rather than only C close or trigger price. A rejected attempt consumes the setup; it is not delayed for another entry-window second. Accepted trades store zoneDistance, and the setting is retained in presets, reports and the report audit. Existing saved configurations lacking the field behave as unlimited.

## Optional major-zone entry filter (engine 0.18.0)

majorZonesOnly is a boolean, default false. Enable Trade major zones only (MDZ / MSZ) beside the maximum-entry-distance setting to restrict Z/R/C/E setups to the lowest demand bottom and highest supply top confirmed so far that day. Equal levels keep the earliest zone. Historical major flags at formation do not override the current extremes, and later zones cannot retroactively affect earlier setups. Existing active-zone/overlap rules and first-retest eligibility still apply. If a zone loses its major role before entry, cancel its unfinished setup even if it remains an active non-major extreme. Positions already open keep their normal exits. This entry filter does not alter the chart’s historical zone display or candle exit-target selection. The flag is included in presets, saved runs and the report audit.

## NO LIMIT runners and first-target R/R (engine 0.19.0)

Custom exits support type noLimit with a required positive trailing moveStop rule. The editor automatically selects and locks trailing protection for that row. Its percentage still contributes to the mandatory 100% allocation. No synthetic TP is assigned: target/rawTarget remain null in saved plans. Each runner trails only its allocated shares from entry, using the existing next-one-second-sample tightening policy and the favorable SPY extreme. The original/shared protective stop also applies, with the tighter protection governing a runner. Multiple runner rows can have independent trailing distances. Price-based TP rows continue to exit their own allocations, and runner shares cannot be sold by a TP belonging to another row. Standard gap, costs, original-stop, session-close and data-end rules remain applicable. A runner is labeled NO LIMIT and its separate trailing-stop history is shown on the chart.

R/R now uses the FIRST price-based target in configured strategy order, at both signal and actual fill, irrespective of that exit’s sell percentage or subsequent exits. Its effective 5-cent meet-target margin is included. A runner leg does not disable this filter when a price-based target exists. An all-runner plan has no fixed reward/RR and records rr=null rather than inventing unlimited profit or an infinite target. Cash P&L always derives from actual fills, not the planned R/R reference.

## Selected entry trends (engine 0.20.0)

The top of Strategy rules has Uptrend, Downtrend and Sideways checkboxes. allowUptrend, allowDowntrend and allowSideways each default true; any combination, including all off, is valid. These settings filter the recorded confirmed trend at the entry signal independently of the existing trend-to-direction filter. Unchecking a trend blocks both CALLS and PUTS in that trend; when the direction filter is enabled it still restricts Uptrend to CALLS and Downtrend to PUTS. Blocked first-retest setups are consumed. All off prevents every entry. With any checkbox off, unclassified/unclear entries are also blocked. With all on, existing behavior is preserved, including legacy unclear entries when the original trend filter is disabled. Selections are validated as booleans and retained in presets, saved runs and the report audit.

## Editable trading-start time (engine 0.21.0)

tradingStart defaults to 10:30, one hour after the normal 09:30 market open. Start trading (New York time) is editable in the main Strategy rules section. It accepts HH:MM within the configured session, independently of sessionStart. New entry fills are permitted at or after tradingStart and strictly before session close minus 60 minutes, with date-specific early closes honored. This replaces the old fixed opening 30-minute restriction. Zone formation and trend warmup still use sessionStart. Missing opening data does not shift tradingStart. First retests consumed before trading starts are not queued for another attempt. Existing saved reports retain their original results; new runs and presets save the chosen clock time. New York daylight-saving rules govern all displayed/session times.

## Delayed mixed-strategy runner activation (engine 0.22.0)

When a strategy has a price-based TP plus NO LIMIT runners, runner trails activate only when the FIRST configured price-based TP actually fills. Before that, all runner shares retain the original/shared protective stop; pre-activation favorable highs/lows cannot tighten the runner. Activation anchors at the TP fill’s SPY price and then uses favorable extremes from that sample onward, with the existing next-sample stop-update policy. The first exit’s break-even/other shared stop action still applies to remaining shares. If the TP never fills, runner trails never activate. Pure runner-only strategies without a TP retain entry-time activation. Chart and fill-detail exit numbers now use configured exit index; a combined protective exit is labeled Remaining position rather than incorrectly borrowing a configured TP number.

## Optional repeated zone trades (engine 0.23.0)

Allow repeated zone trades (allowZoneReuse) defaults false and appears in the main Strategy rules section. Off preserves the existing first-touch-only opportunity: failed, blocked and successful attempts spend the zone. On allows a later touch of the same still-active zone to start another Z/R/C/E sequence after earlier attempts or completed trades. Existing cooldown, active-zone replacement, optional MDZ/MSZ filter, trend/time/distance/RR checks, and one-open-position rule remain in force. A replaced zone cannot be revived by this switch. The value is validated as a boolean and retained in presets, saved runs and the report audit.

## Minimum profit-target distance (engine 0.24.0)

minProfitTarget defaults to zero and is editable in the main Strategy rules section. It is a non-negative dollar amount of favorable SPY movement per share, not total position P&L. For the original strategy use its planned target; for a custom exit plan use the first price-based TP in strategy order, matching the R/R reference. Meet targets use their executable level after the five-cent margin. Require target distance >= the configured minimum. Check at the completed C signal before arming the trigger, and again against the actual cost-adjusted fill to account for gaps, spread and slippage. Too-small targets reject the setup under minimum profit target; no entry is queued for later. A positive minimum also rejects runner-only plans that cannot demonstrate a fixed target distance. Zero preserves their previous behavior. Selections travel with presets, saved runs and report audit.

Trend update: use the last five completed one-minute VWAP samples, updated every minute. Slope is endpoint change divided by the four elapsed minutes between those samples. Two consecutive classifications confirm a change; earliest confirmation is 09:36. The existing slope threshold applies. Premarket remains excluded.

VWAP between entry and TP: ignore (default), skip trade, or cap fixed targets at completed-minute VWAP available before entry. Strictly intervening levels only. Each affected fixed exit is capped; no-limit runners remain unchanged. Recheck minimum reward/risk and minimum profit target after adjustment and actual entry costs.

### Stacked candles (optional)
Off by default. Allowed overlap defaults to 1 cent of SPY price. For CALLs, the confirmation low must be at least reversal close minus the margin; for PUTs, confirmation high must be at most reversal close plus the margin. Entry uses the confirmation close as the same boundary. Observed entry-window samples are checked until fill; an opening fill checks its open, without using later price action. For intrasecond triggers the full observed one-second range must satisfy the boundary because its ordering is unknown. Ignored dojis retain the actual reversal reference. Settings persist with the last run and presets.

Confirmation candle must not touch the zone: optional, off by default. When enabled, a CALL confirmation low must be strictly above the demand zone top; a PUT confirmation high must be strictly below the supply zone bottom. Wick or exact boundary contact disqualifies confirmation.

### Candle exit target scope correction
Both 1m and 5m candle meet/max exits use only the excursion after the selected entry zone was confirmed and before its current retest began. Both candles of a target pair must start at or after that zone confirmation (or the preceding departure touch, if later), and the pair must finish by retest start. Source/formation candles and unrelated earlier-day levels cannot supply the target. The farthest body turnaround in that excursion sets meet, using the close/open nearest entry; max uses its wick. A missing target skips the trade. Existing five-cent meet exit margin, costs, intervening-zone/VWAP caps and R/R checks still apply.
